Press Tab and Shift+Tab to move between nodes in reporting order. Press Enter on a node to select it, or Ctrl+Enter to select it and open the properties panel. Press Escape to clear the selection. Press Space to expand or collapse the focused node. Use Shift+arrow keys to move between nodes, and Alt+arrow keys to add a node next to the focused one. Arrow keys on their own move the selected node on the canvas, or pan the canvas when nothing is selected. Press Delete to remove the selected node.
