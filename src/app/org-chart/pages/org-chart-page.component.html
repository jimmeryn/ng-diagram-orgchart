Use the arrow keys to move the selected node on the canvas. Use Shift+arrow keys to move selection between nodes. Use Alt+arrow keys to add a new node next to the focused node (down or right for a child; left/right or up/down for a sibling, depending on layout). Press Enter to open the properties panel for the focused node. Press Space to expand or collapse the focused node. Press Delete to remove the selected node.
